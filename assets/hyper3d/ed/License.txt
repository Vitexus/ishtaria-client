Gazelle, onager, goat, lizard, turtle, falcon, pigeon and tamarisk meshes generated for Ishtaria with Hyper3D Rodin (Gen-2.5).

  The generated mesh was a bare trunk and branches. The shipped GLB adds a crown of foliage tufts.

Each Rodin GLB is grounded, scaled to a real height (gazelle 1.05 m, onager 1.35 m, goat 0.90 m, lizard 0.28 m, turtle 0.22 m, falcon 0.45 m, pigeon 0.25 m, tamarisk 1.40 m) and carries looping Idle and Walk clips (a small bob; the meshes are not skeletal). They are catalog scenery, not inventory items. Released with the client under the MIT license.
