Terrain albedos of 0 A.D.
========================

Maps: desert_dirt_persia_1, desert_dirt_persia_2, desert_dirt_persia_rocks,
desert_dirt_persia_rocky, desert_cliff_persia_1, desert_cliff_persia_2,
desert_cliff_persia_base, desert_plants_b_persia, persia_dirt_plants_01,
desert_farmland, desert_city_tile_pers.

Changes: converted with tools/zeroad-terrain.py from cached DDS/PNG of the 0ad-data package to PNG
at most 1024 px. The client detail shader blends desert_dirt_persia_1 into sand,
persia_dirt_plants_01 into soil and desert_cliff_persia_1 into rock (`use_persia_textures`,
`persia_blend` ≈ 0.35); Poly Haven layers remain the base.
