Fauna of 0 A.D.
===============

Models: gazelle, goat, sheep, cow, bull, donkey, pig, chicken, fox, wolf, lion, lioness, deer,
boar, crocodile, camel, horse_persian, zebu, mastiff, hawk, crow, seagull, fish_generic.

Author and copyright: Wildfire Games (http://www.wildfiregames.com/), 0 A.D. art,
https://gitlab.com/0ad/0ad (art/meshes/skeletal/*, art/textures/skins/skeletal/*,
art/animation/*, actors under art/actors/fauna/).

These files are modifications of the originals and are released under the same licence (CC BY-SA 3.0).

Changes: converted with tools/zeroad-fauna-anim.py from compiled PSMD meshes and PSSA animations
to glTF with Idle and Walk clips. Birds map glide→Idle and flight→Walk; fish maps two idle clips.
Mastiff Walk uses the 0 A.D. run clip. Catalogue ids: `zeroad.<name>`.

Do not re-run tools/zeroad-scenery.py over these files.
